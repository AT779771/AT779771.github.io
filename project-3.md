# Project 3: Creative Problem-Solving & Prototyping

## Overview
This project focuses on experimentation, iteration, and turning ideas into improved solutions. It is rooted in the belief that good work often begins with exploration, feedback, and revision.

## Purpose
The goal is to practice thinking in a flexible and creative way while producing results that are useful and intentional. This project reflects my interest in solving problems through structured experimentation.

## Process

1. Identify a problem or opportunity
2. Brainstorm possible approaches
3. Test a concept or rough prototype
4. Review what worked and what did not
5. Improve the design and repeat the cycle

## Skills Demonstrated

- creative thinking
- iterative design
- planning and refinement
- adapting based on feedback
- communicating ideas clearly

## What I Learned

The most important lesson from this work is that progress often comes from trying, adjusting, and learning quickly. Strong ideas are not always finalized on the first attempt; they become clearer through iteration.
